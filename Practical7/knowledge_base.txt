Company Handbook: Nimbus Cloud Solutions 
About Nimbus Cloud Solutions 
Nimbus Cloud Solutions was founded in 2016 and provides cloud infrastructure and DevOps automation tools for small and medium-sized businesses. The company is headquartered in Austin, Texas, and has remote employees across 12 countries. Nimbus serves over 3,000 customers, ranging from early-stage startups to mid-market enterprises. 
Product Overview 
Nimbus offers three core products: 
1. NimbusDeploy - a one-click deployment platform that supports Docker, Kubernetes, and serverless functions. 
2. NimbusMonitor - a real-time observability suite offering log aggregation, metrics dashboards, and automated alerting. 
3. NimbusVault - a secrets management and encryption service used to store API keys, credentials, and certificates securely. 
Pricing Plans 
Nimbus offers four pricing tiers: 
- Starter: Free for up to 2 projects, includes basic monitoring. 
- Growth: $49/month per project, includes NimbusDeploy and NimbusMonitor. - Scale: $199/month per project, adds NimbusVault and priority support. - Enterprise: Custom pricing, includes dedicated infrastructure, SSO, and a named customer success manager. 
Support Policy 
Standard support is available via email with a 24-hour response time for Growth and Scale plans. Enterprise customers receive 24/7 phone and chat support with a guaranteed 1-hour response time for critical incidents. All plans include access to the community forum and public documentation. 
Security and Compliance 
Nimbus is SOC 2 Type II certified and undergoes annual third-party penetration testing. All customer data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.3. Nimbus is GDPR compliant and offers data residency options in the US, EU, and APAC regions for Enterprise customers. 
Onboarding Process 
New customers go through a four-step onboarding process: 
1. Account setup and team invitation. 
2. Connecting a cloud provider (AWS, GCP, or Azure). 
3. Deploying a first project using a guided template.
4. Configuring monitoring and alert thresholds. 
The typical onboarding process takes less than 30 minutes for Starter and Growth customers, and is supported by a dedicated onboarding specialist for Enterprise customers. 
Refund Policy 
Nimbus offers a 14-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans. Refund requests must be submitted through the billing dashboard or by emailing billing@nimbuscloud.example. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days. 
Company Values 
Nimbus operates according to four core values: Customer Obsession, Bias for Action, Radical Transparency, and Sustainable Growth. These values guide hiring decisions, product roadmap prioritization, and internal performance reviews.
